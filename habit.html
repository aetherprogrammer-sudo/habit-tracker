About Ethio2030

We are dedicated to fostering holistic national progress through youth-driven technology initiatives, collaborative learning, and leadership mentorship.

Our Mission

To equip Ethiopian youth with digital capabilities, problem-solving mindsets, and sustainable leadership opportunities.

Our Vision

A technologically empowered, self-sustaining, and prosperous Ethiopia leading East Africa in innovation by 2030.

Our Impact

Uniting thousands of ambitious students, mentors, tech enthusiasts, and community builders across the country.
